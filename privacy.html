Decker Agent

Privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

Decker Agent is a personally operated Hermes assistant for its owner's use. This policy describes this deployment's handling of Google user data.

Access and use

With explicit OAuth consent, the assistant can access Gmail messages and mailbox information, calendars and events, Drive files, Google Docs and Sheets, and contacts. It uses this access to carry out the owner's requests, such as finding information, summarizing content, managing events, or creating and editing documents. The granted permissions may also permit sending mail and deleting resources.

Processing and storage

The assistant runs on an AWS-hosted server. OAuth credentials are stored outside the source repository with restricted filesystem permissions. Task content may be retained in the assistant's conversation history, memory or workspace. Host and infrastructure administrators may have technical access to the deployment.

Content needed to answer a request may be processed by the configured AI model provider. Requests and responses sent through WhatsApp are also handled by that messaging service. Those services process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Google data is not sold or used by the operator for advertising or to train generalized AI models.

Google API data

Decker Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the personal assistant features requested by its owner.

Retention and control

Credentials and task records remain in the deployment until removed by its owner. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously saved conversation or workspace data. The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections and remove local credentials, histories, memories and files from the deployment.

Contact

Contact the operator through the site repository. Do not include credentials or private information in public issues.
