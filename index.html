Decker Agent

A privately operated personal assistant powered by Hermes Agent. It helps its owner search information, manage personal tasks and interact with Google Workspace through explicitly authorized access.

This application is for its owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept registrations.

With the owner's authorization, the assistant can work with Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and read-only Contacts. Access can include reading, sending, creating, editing and deleting resources according to the Google consent screen.
